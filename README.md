## 🚀 Featured Project

### 💰 IndusWealth — Unified Financial Management & Debt Optimization

> Your entire financial life, one dashboard. IndusWealth connects all your bank accounts through Plaid for a real-time, 360° view of net worth and liquidity — then puts your money to work.

🔗 **[induswealth.app](https://induswealth.app)** · 📱 Launching soon on Google Play

**Highlights**

- 🏦 Secure, read-only **Plaid** bank aggregation across all your accounts
- 📊 Net worth & liquid cash dashboards with advanced analytics
- ⚔️ **Debt Attack Plan** — a prioritization engine that charts the optimal payoff path for high-interest debt
- 🐶 **Watchdog** — hunts down forgotten subscriptions draining your wallet every month
- 🎯 Savings goals and smart transaction categorization
